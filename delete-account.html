Delete Account - Duoit

If you wish to delete your Duoit account and associated data, please read this document carefully. Your account and all related information can be permanently deleted from our systems.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you request to delete your Duoit account, the following data associated with your account will be permanently removed:

  • Your user account and authentication credentials
  • Your username, email address, and profile information
  • All personal and legal information associated with your account
  • Your activity logs and statistics
  • Any personal preferences and settings
  • Achievement badges and progress records

What Your Partner Can Still See

If you share an account or activity with a partner, they will retain access to:

  • Photos and media shared within your partnership
  • Trees and forest data you planted or contributed to together
  • Tasks and shared activities you completed together

However, your name and personal identification will be removed from these shared items, and your partner will see them as contributed anonymously or by the partnership account.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We cannot restore your data after deletion.

Data That May Be Retained

In some cases, certain data may be retained for legal, compliance, or operational purposes:

  • Anonymized analytics data (cannot be tied back to you)
  • Transaction logs required for financial compliance (if applicable)
  • Data required by law enforcement or legal obligations
  • Backup copies may be retained for a limited period but will eventually be purged

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your Duoit account:

  1. Open the Duoit app on your device
  2. Navigate to your Profile or Settings
  3. Look for "Delete Account" option in the settings menu
  4. Tap on "Delete Account"
  5. Review the confirmation message carefully
  6. Confirm your decision to proceed with account deletion
  7. Your account will be deleted immediately and permanently
Note: After deletion, you will be logged out of all devices where you were signed in to your Duoit account.

Verification and Security

For security purposes, you may be required to:

  • Confirm your email address
  • Verify your identity through email verification
  • Provide additional authentication if necessary

Alternative to Deletion

If you're having issues with your account or have concerns, please consider:

  • Temporarily disabling account notifications in your settings
  • Contacting our support team for assistance
  • Reviewing our Privacy Policy for more information about data usage

Questions or Concerns

If you have any questions about the account deletion process or concerns about your data, please contact us at: support@duoit.net

We're here to help and would love to know if there's anything we can do to improve your experience with Duoit.
